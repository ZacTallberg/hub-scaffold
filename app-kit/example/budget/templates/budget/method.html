{{ s.total }} open line{{ s.total|pluralize }}, and the four rules that count them

  1. Open lines are every line not archived. Archived lines are counted separately ({{ s.archived }} now) and never mixed in.
  2. Over budget is the line's recorded status, set by the team that owns it, not recomputed here.
  3. Measured means an actual has been entered. A line with no actual shows a dash, never 0, and has no variance.
  4. Variance is actual minus planned, measured lines only. Nothing on these pages is modelled or estimated.

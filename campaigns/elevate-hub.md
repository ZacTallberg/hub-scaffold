# ELEVATE — make a Hub exceptional without breaking its truth

Read `00-orchestration-method.md`, then `../PROJECT/HUB-QUALITY.md`. This campaign is required for a
new Hub surface or a material visual, interaction, realtime, flow, or coordination redesign.

1. **Research** current primary standards and the project's users, identity, constraints, and live
   transport. Record sources and decisions in `PROJECT/research/`.
2. **Audit rendered reality** at the relevant widths, inputs, motion preferences, and
   empty/ordinary/dense/live/degraded/error states. Record concrete product defects, not adjectives.
3. **Brief** the intended hierarchy, spatial rhythm, semantic tokens, motion grammar, state language,
   performance budgets, and preservation of local identity.
4. **Build** from canonical state through the renderer. Keep truth, accessibility, delivery states,
   and transport degradation explicit throughout, not as a final polish pass.
5. **Attempt the real experience** on the affected paths and retain the operation receipt. Do not
   create tests or validation rituals for copy, wording, color, spacing, visual style, ordinary
   animation, or another non-critical UI fix.
6. **Use a transient probe only for a critical boundary** such as authorization, destructive data,
   migration, protocol compatibility, or concurrency/fencing. Run it once from temporary storage,
   keep its result, and delete the probe before commit.
7. **Crystallize** reusable improvements upstream as curated, scrubbed units. Never bulk-merge an
   instance or overwrite its brand/theme during an upgrade.

Completed child task receipts compose into the elevation receipt. Do not rerun them. A release may
exercise only the genuinely new seam created by their integration, and a closer may never nest
another closer or suite.

**Exit and stop:** the new or materially elevated Hub is complete when the affected real experience
works, its truthful receipt is recorded, and any rare critical probe has been removed. If no critical
boundary was crossed, stop there—do not add tests, screenshots-as-gates, copy assertions, or an
independent verification ceremony. If the real attempt fails, record the observation as fresh task
input for possible routing to a repair/error-fixing lane; the delivery agent does not speculate or
silently become that repair agent.
